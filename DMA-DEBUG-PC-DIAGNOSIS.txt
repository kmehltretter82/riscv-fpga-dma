Both exact images place dma_debug_init at 0xffffffff8005ad0c.  At cycles
88,000,000 through 91,000,000 their committed PCs repeatedly included

At cycles 92,000,000 and 93,000,000 the heartbeat sampled exception-return
code, while a cycle-94,000,000 sample was again in page allocation.  Those
sparse samples prove entry into the named DMA-debug initialization code, but
do not prove the initcall finished.  More importantly, their continuing
allocator trace led to inspection of the exact kernel configurations.  Both
contain CONFIG_CMDLINE_FORCE=y and a compiled command line without
dma_debug_entries=1024, so Linux ignored the option present only in the DT.
The runs were stopped and checksum-sealed as interrupted diagnostics.  They
are not optimized acceptance evidence.  The corrected build recipes put the
option in CONFIG_CMDLINE and assert that exact value after olddefconfig.

The option does not disable DMA API debugging: CONFIG_DMA_API_DEBUG, the
DMA-debug error counter, and the final test gates remain mandatory.
